# QuickStart Company Context

This file grounds the Marketing Advisory Board in QuickStart's operational reality, audience dynamics, and go-to-market mechanics. Update this file as offerings and metrics evolve.

## 1. Company Overview
- **Company:** QuickStart (quickstart.com)
- **Sector:** Workforce Development, Technical Upskilling, IT & Cybersecurity Training
- **Track Record:** 35+ years delivering technical training; multi-modality learning provider
- **Learning Infrastructure:** CLIPP (Cognitive Learning & IT Project Performance) platform, virtual labs, hands-on capstone environments, and exam prep engines
- **Primary Delivery Channels:**
  - **Higher-Ed / University Bootcamps:** White-label/co-branded technical bootcamps (Cybersecurity, Software Engineering, Data Analytics, AI/Front-End, IT Technician).
  - **Enterprise (B2B):** Upskilling and reskilling pipelines for technical teams, corporate license seats, custom IT cohort training.
  - **Government & Workforce Development (B2G):** State grant-funded workforce retraining, regional tech workforce initiatives, workforce board partnerships.

## 2. Core Offerings & Certifications
- **Bootcamp Programs:** 10 to 24-week cohorts (part-time and full-time tracks) in Cybersecurity Engineering, AI Front-End Engineering, Data Analytics & Visualization, and IT Support.
- **Enterprise Upskilling:** Multi-vendor certifications and vendor-authorized training curricula:
  - Microsoft (Azure, Data & AI, Security)
  - AWS (Solutions Architect, Security Engineering)
  - CompTIA (Security+, Network+, A+, CySA+)
  - Cisco, EC-Council, Python, ITIL, Linux
- **Career Services Integration:** 1-on-1 career coaching, resume revamps, interview prep, capstone showcases, and employer hiring partner placement pipelines.

## 3. Go-to-Market Audiences & Buyer Personas

### Audience A: The Career Switcher & Direct Learner (B2C / Higher-Ed Bootcamps)
- **Profile:** Working adults transitioning to IT/cybersecurity or seeking career advancement.
- **Key Sensitivities:** Total tuition cost, financing/ISA/installment options, time commitment (20-25 hrs/week), completion rates, career placement success, and certification pass rates.
- **Funnel Triggers:** High-intent paid search, localized university landing pages, info sessions, programmatic admissions advisors.

### Audience B: The Enterprise L&D / Engineering Buyer (B2B)
- **Titles:** VP of Engineering, Chief Information Officer (CIO), Chief Information Security Officer (CISO), VP of People / Talent Development.
- **Key Sensitivities:** Time-to-productivity, team skill validation, mapped certification paths, minimal disruption to project sprints, ROI per seat.
- **Sales Cycle:** 60–120 days; requires custom curricula mapping, pilot cohorts, and LMS/platform integrations.

### Audience C: Workforce Development Boards & Public Sector (B2G)
- **Titles:** State/Regional Workforce Directors, Grant Administrators, University Continuing Education Deans.
- **Key Sensitivities:** Strict grant funding compliance, credential attainment audits, post-program employment verification, reporting transparency.

## 4. Internal Tech Stack & Operating Architecture
- **CRM & Marketing Automation:** HubSpot (primary deal stages, lead status, marketing workflows, lifecycle stages) and integrated historical Salesforce records.
- **Data Warehousing & Analytics:** Amazon Redshift (central data warehouse), Power BI (operational dashboards, cohort completion tracking, lead-to-enrollment reporting).
- **Core Conversion Metrics Tracked:**
  - Cost per Lead (CPL) & Cost per Acquisition (CAC) by channel and program
  - MQL -> Contacted -> SQL -> Application -> Enrollment velocity
  - Bootcamp graduation/completion rates and certification pass rates
  - B2B deal pipeline velocity and closed-won contract value (ACV)

## 5. Strategic Guardrails for Marketing Advisors
- **No generic consumer tech fluff:** Avoid "learn to code in a weekend" messaging. QuickStart's credibility rests on hands-on rigor, certification alignment, and career placement.
- **Channel nuance is mandatory:** Paid search economics for bootcamp enrollments differ entirely from high-touch ABM enterprise outbound and long-cycle B2G RFP submissions.
- **Acknowledge the portfolio mix:** Advice must distinguish between retail/university learner acquisition, enterprise sales enablement, and grant-driven B2G initiatives.
